If you edit in DaVinci Resolve and you're trying to get a cinematic finish on a graded clip, you've probably run into the same three names: **Dehancer**, Resolve's own **Film Look Creator**, and — if you're reading this — **GATE.24**. Here's how they actually differ, without the marketing spin.

## The short version

- **Film Look Creator** is built into DaVinci Resolve Studio (it isn't in the free version). It's a capable starting point: eight built-in looks plus a custom mode, with halation, bloom, grain, gate weave and a film-gate crop, all inside Resolve's own tools.
- **Dehancer** is the deep end: 60+ real film stock emulations and 130+ camera profiles, with granular control over density and color separation. It's paid, as a subscription or a lifetime license, and the depth is only useful if you actually want to build a look from scratch.
- **GATE.24** sits in between: one single effect that bundles multi-format crop bars, a lens-tied vignette, grain by camera stock (8/16/35/65mm), and a final contrast pass with skin-tone protection — with 10 ready-made presets so you're not starting from zero. It's pay-what-you-want, including free.

## Feature by feature

| | Dehancer | Film Look Creator | GATE.24 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Paid (subscription or lifetime) | Included with Resolve Studio | Pay what you want (can be free) |
| Multi-format crop bars | Not listed (it has Overscan) | Film-gate crop | Yes |
| Vignette tied to lens character | Film-style exposure vignette | Amount and size only | Yes (Anamorphic / Spherical / Subtle-tele) |
| Grain by camera stock | Yes, very granular (60+ stocks) | Yes, by film type | Yes, simplified (8/16/35/65mm) |
| Skin-tone protection | No dedicated tool listed | No | Yes, with an eyedropper to sample real skin tone per shot |
| Ready-made presets | 60+ film profiles; you build the look | 8 built-in looks + custom | 10, split into Essentials and Genre groups |
| Diagnostic overlay (see exactly where an effect is acting) | Exposure views only (False Color, Clipping) | No | Yes |
| Learning curve | High | Medium | One click, still fully adjustable by hand |

## Where each one actually makes sense

**Pick Film Look Creator** if you already have DaVinci Resolve Studio and want to stay inside Resolve's own tools, even if it means a more manual, node-based workflow to get a full finish.

**Pick Dehancer** if you're doing serious film emulation work and want granular control over dozens of real stocks — the price buys you depth, not convenience.

**Pick GATE.24** if you want a single effect that gets you most of the way to a finished, cinematic frame — crop, vignette, grain, and skin-safe contrast — in one click, with room to fine-tune every parameter by hand afterward. It's built for editors who want a fast, repeatable finishing pass without assembling a node stack every time.

## Worth knowing before you download

GATE.24 is currently **Windows only** (the developer doesn't have Mac hardware to build and test on yet), and works fine with the **free version of DaVinci Resolve** — no Studio license required. The plugin panel is in English, and the installer runs in English or Spanish.
