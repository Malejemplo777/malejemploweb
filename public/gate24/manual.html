Quick-start guide
GATE.24

The cinematic finish for DaVinci Resolve — crop, vignette, film grain, contrast, weave and flicker, in one node.

by Malejemplo

01

Install it

1
Close Resolve So the plugin copies cleanly.
2
Unzip, run Install.bat Accept the Windows admin prompt.
3
Open Resolve GATE.24 is on the Color page, under OpenFX → Malejemplo.

Windows only for now. Works on Resolve Free and Studio. To remove it, run Uninstall.bat.

If Windows asks you to confirm an unknown publisher, that's expected — there's no code signing yet. The scripts are plain text: open them in Notepad first if you like.

02

Put it last

GATE.24 does its math on real, developed color — never on Log or a wide gamut. One node, one fixed spot in the chain.

Not before the grade, not on Log. Skin protection and grain both read the wrong values.
03

Apply it, pick a look

1
Color page Open the clip you already graded.
2
OpenFX → Malejemplo Find GATE.24 in the effects list.
3
Drop on your last node Or a fresh one right after it.
Essentials Cinema 35 · Cinema 65 · Editorial 4:3 · Urban Editorial
Looks Dusty Western · Romance / Reverie · Homegrown VHS · Classic Horror · Worn-Out VHS · Terror Found Footage

Pick one from either list. It sets the format, switches on Crop, Vignette, Grain and Contrast, and turns Weave and Flicker on or off to suit the look. It never touches your Reframe. Picking from one list resets the other. Tweak anything afterwards, and press Reset preset to go back to the look.

04

The panel

Simple mode shows the presets and six switches. Turn one on and its main slider appears right under it. Flip on Advanced to see more settings for every effect that is on. It always opens in Simple.

Presets Pick a look, tweak anything after.
Crop Bars, any format.
Weave The picture jumps a hair every frame, under the bars.
Flicker Exposure pulses frame to frame, like a projector.
Vignette Darkens or lifts the edges.
Grain Real stocks: 8/16/35/65mm.
Contrast S-curve with skin protection.
Reframe Move the shot, not the frame.
05

When to open Advanced

Advanced only adds more settings to the effects you have switched on, right under their slider.

  • Crop Format is always there. Advanced adds bar thickness V and H, offset, feather, corner roundness, bar colour and opacity.
  • Vignette To pick the Optics (anamorphic, spherical, tele) or soften the edge.
  • Grain To change Stock, Size, Color or Animation — if the grain reads chunky on your footage, lower Size.
  • Contrast When skin looks pushed: change Protection or sample the Skin Tone eyedropper. Use Preview → Skin/Midtones to see what is protected.
  • Weave Horizontal jump, Rate (every frame or every 2) and Seed. Most looks only need Vertical.
  • Flicker Seed, to get a different flicker sequence. Amount is always in Simple.
06

Remember

Presets apply once. Change values by hand afterward. Reset preset takes you back to the look.
Updates don't reach old clips. Re-pick the preset on a clip to bring it the new values.
Any resolution, proxies included. 720p, 1080p, 4K — same math, same look.
07

If something fails

  • Nothing happens Everything starts Off. Pick a preset, or turn on an Enable.
  • Not in the list Close Resolve, run Install.bat again, then look under Malejemplo in Color → OpenFX.
  • Install won't run Close Resolve first: the script stops if it is open.
  • Windows warning “Unknown publisher” is expected (no code signing). Accept it.
  • Looks wrong GATE.24 goes last, after the grade and in Rec.709. On Log footage it will look wrong.
  • Picture edge shows Raise Reframe Zoom a little, or lower Weave Vertical.
  • Slow playback Use Render Cache or proxies. Grain is the heaviest effect: turn it off while editing.
